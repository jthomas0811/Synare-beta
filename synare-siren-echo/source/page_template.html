Synare, SE-1 Siren & Echo

  • SYNARE pad: tap to strike (higher hits harder), slide for pitch, drag up or down for decay, a second finger for wobble.
  • Tap TRIG to switch the siren on.
  • Hold SIGNAL and SIREN. Two fingers work.
  • Tap a number to set BASE, MODE and BEAT. Drag up or down on VOLUME.
  • MOM: on while you hold TRIG. TOGGLE: each tap switches it.
  • Tap a preset to recall it. Hold one to store.
  • The siren and the Synare both run into the echo. Slide ECHO DELAY while it plays. Drag up or down on ECHO REPEAT; past 9 it runs away.

Plays over your music: start Spotify or Apple Music first, then siren on top. On iPhone, if there is no sound, check the silent switch.
